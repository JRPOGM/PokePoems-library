# The Crow That Lingers Overhead

<blockquote>
The crow circles once over my head<br>
Sunlight catches her obsidian feathers<br>
Onyx eyes bore down upon me<br>
There is a chip in her beak<br>
A golden tooth missing from her lower jaw<br>
“How did you get that one?” I ask her<br>
She croaks one time

The crow circles over my head again<br>
A feather slips from her wings<br>
Flutters onto my book<br>
There is already a pen dipped with ink<br>
A sketch of her form like an arrow, beneath her quill<br>
“Would I lose too much detail if I colored you in?” I ask her<br>
She croaks a second time

The crow makes her third flight overhead<br>
Diving to perch on the gutter<br>
Talons scratch the tin<br>
There is a tilt she does with her head<br>
A stare I meet with my own eyes<br>
“How does the world look to you?” I ask her
She croaks a final time,

“Slow. Decrepit. Dying.<br>
So short is everything else,<br>
Searching for variations of immortality.<br>
Only I remain, each time.<br>
To you, I am blackness.<br>
Dark. Mysterious. Familiar.<br>
You are food I await.<br>
I see the seconds over your head.<br>
The caws of your clock counting down.<br>
I wish you luck, frail creature,<br>
To live a fulfilling life.<br>
You will be delicious in death.”
</blockquote>

## Murkrow, the Darkness Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/murkrow.jpeg)

> “_There are only a handful of Pokémon species worldwide associated with back luck and misfortunes, and Murkrow is one of the more common species believed to bring it to others. Many still believe it curses those who cross its path, though the species is known to encourage people to chase it away, evidently leading those who do into confusing, dense wildness to strand them in isolation. Because of the mischief it intentionally causes, Murkrow gained a sour reputation amongst many populated cities, eventually leading to the development and encouragement of treating the Pokémon as a cursed spirit and doing what they can to ward them off from civilizations entirely. In more rural regions like Johto, some cities partake in spiritual practices to teach travelers how to ward off passing Murkrow in the adventures._”

> “_Domesticated Murkrow are another form of trouble entirely. Conditioned out of their habits to toy with others and put them in unfortunate situations, their bad behavior finds life in their love for thievery. They have a similar tendency to the Meowth line, fascinated with shiny and sparkling objects, plucking them when they can and storing them away in secret stashes; both the bird and cat species of Pokémon have been known to fight when one stumbles upon the other’s hoard. More troubling than the Meowth, though, is the Murkrow’s preference for jewelry -- necklaces, bands and rings -- that lead to relationship trouble even between people the Murkrow does not know personally._”
