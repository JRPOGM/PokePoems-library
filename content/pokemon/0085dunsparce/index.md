# Come On In

<blockquote>
Just because I hide inside all day<br>
Does not mean I am avoiding you

There’s comfort in the burrow<br>
In the space I make for myself<br>
When it is on me to let people in<br>
It is confidence in control

The door remains unlocked<br>
Do not be as afraid as you assumed of me
</blockquote>

## Dunsparce, the Land Snake Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/dunsparce.jpeg)

> “_It is a rare occurrence to cross paths with a Dunsparce. Once believed to be a species capable of proper flight and could soar the skies above, now the species is predominantly an underground dweller, rarely traveling outside of caves and under sunlight. They still have their wings, shrunken and weak on their backs, barely capable of carrying them off the ground for more than a minute at a time. Instead, they get around slithering on their bellies, or more commonly digging backwards with their drill-shaped tail, leaving behind long and winding webs of caverns to disorient any who would chase them underground._”

> “_It is the Dunsparce’s instinct to run at the first sight of any approaching other -- Pokémon or human -- when and wherever possible. It is considered extremely lucky to even get a few feet close to one before they scurry off into their underground hidings. One of the only species Dunsparce do not run from are Diglett. As a fellow burrowing creature, leaving tunnels in their wake wherever they dig, both species of Pokémon share their caverns with the other for living and traveling through; though, the size of Diglett’s population in any region vastly outnumbers and pocket of Dunsparce in any of their habitats._”
