# A Snake That Cuts His Fangs

<blockquote>
Why do I continue to carry this poison?<br>
As any other, I am made of mistakes.<br>
Memories of the faces I’ve wronged, seared.<br>
Actions of harm, carved.<br>
As any other, they had molded me.<br>
Ugly and warped, still cutting off clumps.<br>
Words and choices, understanding what does not sink the heart.<br>
As any other, I have tried to learn from them.<br>
Right and wrong.<br>
For me and them.

So why does it not go away?<br>
Venom infused in my nails.<br>
Toxins coating my teeth.<br>
I have stumbled, yet I continue to trip.<br>
I remain on the path where I cannot walk a straight.<br>
When I could veer off onto solid ground.<br>
Yet I choose the dirt road.

Is this punishment for all the wrongs?<br>
Is growth unobtainable?<br>
Change, impossible?<br>
Happiness, a kind of fever dream?

Am I doing this to myself?<br>
No one else is at the wheel to make this decision.<br>
Am I the one who put nails on this road?<br>
Have I told myself to walk it?

Is this comfort in the familiar?<br>
Staying where I am because I know where it leads,<br>
I know what it can do,<br>
And the choice to walk alone is made in confidence?<br>
Choking on the words I hear bubbling in my throat?<br>
Cutting into my own palms before claws could tear another’s skin?<br>
Have I known the wildebeest too long<br>
To set him free into the wild?

Is this indefinite?<br>
Is this my identity?<br>
Do I risk change?<br>
Or do I remain reigning control?
</blockquote>

## Wooper, the Water Fish/Poison Fish Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/wooper.jpeg)

> “_Wooper are a species that requires hydration to live. These armless Pokémon live in cold climate waters, only traversing on land when the temperatures are sufficiently low enough. They swim in waters using their tails and feet to propel themselves forward, and dig into the mud underwater to sleep. Wooper must consistently stay wet or damp, as any long period of time spent in warmer temperatures and outside of the water can dry their skin quickly and end their lives abruptly. To safely move on land whenever it is not cold enough, each Wooper can coat their bodies with a thin, poisonous film that is harmful to touch for most other species but keeps the Water Fish Pokémon’s skin hydrated until they can wash it off by returning to any body of water._”

> “_This poison coating is how the Paldean Wooper came to be. The waters of the Paldea region are abundant with many different species, but they are home to some of the most aggressive and territorial creatures, constantly fighting each other for dominance and ownership of large spaces on the region’s outskirts or any inland body of water. Long ago, the Wooper line were on the losing side of a territorial dispute and forced to live on land, and to manage their new living conditions they had to continuously apply their poison film to their bodies and each other to survive. After a few generations of struggles and a dwindling population, this endless reapplication of their coating eventually seeped into their DNA and expressed itself in a new offspring of Wooper that lost their Water typing and replaced it to become part Poison instead, until they became they only species of Wooper still living in the Paldea region._”
