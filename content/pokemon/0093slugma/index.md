# Burn Out

<blockquote>
I’m losing parts of myself<br>
Unable to do nothing<br>
Yet immobilized by the thought I can’t do anything<br>
I am burning in these bursts<br>
Boiling alive in a pool of lava I’ve never left<br>
Out and up and every which way I can, I am igniting<br>
A shooting star and a spinning sun, my flames conflicting<br>
Believing I’ll burst before I ever shine
</blockquote>

## Slugma, the Lava Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/slugma.jpeg)

> “_Slugma are one of the only species who live an endlessly awake life. They are always conscious, whether they are moving around or not, as magma coursing through their body to deliver it oxygen and nutrients can only do so as long as they are awake. They do not have a choice, as the pumping of their manga blood keeps their bodies warm and molten, and if they were to stop moving or dip into sleep, the magma would slow and their bodies would harden, breaking off chunks of their form or outright killing the Pokémon if they are immobile for too long._”

> “_This is why Slugma only live in regions of active magma sources. Similar to Magmar, natural lava and magma act as a healing pool for the species, allowing them to keep their bodies warm when they stop moving and to rebuild any part of their bodies they may have lost in prior incidents. Very few Trainers own Slugma of their own, and those who do are all but required to live in an environment that provides sources of natural molten lava to satiate and keep their Pokémon healthy, such as Flannery, a gym leader of the Hoenn region who works at the base of Mt. Chimney in the northwestern area of the region._”
