# What Are They Fighting For?

<blockquote>
Prim and pristine<br>
The blade goes out to war

Sharp and on edge<br>
The blade runs into battle

Chipped and soaked in blood<br>
The blade comes back a broken man<br>
Only to find out they fired the blacksmith
</blockquote>

## Skarmory, the Armor Bird Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/skarmory.jpeg)

> “_Skarmory are lighter than they look. While steel-clad, a Skarmory’s wings are entirely hollow inside each feather that folds under the thin sheath of their wings’ top layer, bringing their overall weight down to just over one hundred pounds. They rely heavily on their wings in battle to cut down their opponents, slashing with the thin tips of their feathers multiple at a time. Though fighting wears and chips and dulls the blades of their wings, and rain and water can rust them easily, Skarmory can shed them at their own volition once a year and grow fresh feathers within a few days. The discarded feathers are usually scattered about without care, and those who stumble upon them usually transform them to be reforged as swords or knives to sell at high prices._”

> “_When Mega Evolved, Skarmory’s armor changes from silver to gold and their method of fighting switches too. Their talons, now enlarged to nearly compete with their wings, become their primary method of attack, only using their wings to kick up winds that blind or unbalance their targets before diving in to cut them up with their claws. Though defensively they are more prone and weak to damage, their speed and power have increased so dramatically that it is near impossible to retaliate against a Mega Skarmory without first putting one’s self in danger._”
