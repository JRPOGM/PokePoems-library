# Crown

<blockquote>
Every man carries a crown<br>
No matter his status, nor determined by his role

It is the way he shapes his hair<br>
Or the angle he tilts the hat he wears<br>
The glass that sits in front of his eyes<br>
Or the glove over his hand raised high

Symbols of power, self-defined<br>
Little monarchs taking the monorail<br>
Kings without their kingdoms acting unrefined<br>
No wine nor grape to drink from missing grails

All in an attempt to display control<br>
Yet to someone else do they bow down
</blockquote>

## Heracross, the Single Horn Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/heracross.jpeg)

> “_Heracross are widely considered to be a sister species to their fellow horned Bug-type Pokémon Pinsir, especially across the neighboring regions of Kanto and Johto where the two are often compared and competing with one another, both in the wild and under human observation. Their bodies and biologies share many similarities, noticeable from the shapes of their bodies are short and stocky, and most notable to both are the horns atop their head. Where Pinsir grow a pair of flexible pincers, Heracross only grow a single, long horn whose crown split at their top end differs between the males and the females of the species. With this horn, Heracross can lift objects one hundred times their weight and throw them with relative ease, being it a chunk of nature or any opponent faced in battle. Their horns also help the species in finding food, able to single out aromas and point Heracross in the direction of which they come from._”

> “_Like the Pinsir species, Heracross are also capable of Mega Evolution, bulking their muscles considerably and increasing their lift strength by five times its previous amount. Even Dynamaxed Pokémon once saved from the species’ power can now be toppled over by a single Mega Heracross. Inversely to their sister species, though, Mega Evolution takes more of a toll on Heracross, easily tiring out their muscles and leaving the Pokémon exhausted and weak afterwards. The pleasure of increasing their powers does not give Heracross the highs of emotion the capability of flight leaves in Pinsir._”
