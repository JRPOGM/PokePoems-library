# The Struggle of Failure

<blockquote>
Am I falling?<br>
Was that the sun?

I constructed these wings<br>
Collected hundreds of feathers<br>
Lathered it all with wax<br>
Strung together in an angel’s image<br>
So why did they fail?

Ambition, too heavy?<br>
Imitation, too vexing?<br>
Insult, too enticing?

Is this plummeting my fault?<br>
Was it out of my hands?

I am falling<br>
I can see the sun shrinking away<br>
Embarrassed by my stare<br>
I watch feathers flip by<br>
Candle wax melting off their tips like tears

It matters not why, I declare<br>
That I did, will be what is remembered<br>
Why, they will only ever assume<br>
And if I do not know why<br>
Who will?
</blockquote>

## Sunkern, the Seed Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/sunkern.jpeg)

> “_Incredibly small and surviving on nothing more than dew drops from the leaves of taller plants, Sunkern are more plant than Pokémon. They do not require any further sustenance beyond dew, only ingesting small amounts of solid food if gifted it by others. As they are more like a static organism, the species is quite weak compared to most every other known Pokémon; they will rarely move on their own accord, and finding one in a different spot than it was earlier is likely due to another species moving them about. Their stagnant lifestyle is intentional, as they intend to maintain the energy they build every day until they evolve, so their next stage of Sunflora can benefit from the power to be strong for them._”

> “_Despite being a pure Grass type and rarely moving on their own accord, there is a strange common occurrence of finding Sunkern falling from the sky. They are not capable of flight like Hoppip, as the pair of leaves over their head are too small and thick for the wind to pick them up. But it is also not proven that they are airborne because of any of the species’ bird Pokémon predators whisking them off and dropping them suddenly; and recorded sighting of this event denies spotting any bird Pokémon in the sky above. As this happens only at the dawn of mornings and no other times of day, some speculate they are being captured by nocturnal Ghost- or Psychic-type Pokémon, but we do not know any species of said types with this behavioral pattern. How a Sunkern finds itself high up in the air to begin with is still a mystery to us._”
