# Born with a Brush

<blockquote>
She is born with bristled fingertips<br>
That dip into others’ skin<br>
Drawing from them pools of their colorful lives

She paints the stories she hears<br>
Pastel people in charcoal civilizations<br>
Canvases covered in tales

And while she sleeps on the shelf<br>
Tucked away with the felt tips collected in the same jar<br>
She dreams pigmented fantasies to bring to life tomorrow
</blockquote>

## Smeargle, the Painter Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/smeargle.jpeg)

> “_Smeargle are natural artists. Their tails are long, the fur on their tips wider and pulled together at the end like the tip of a brush, and from there the species secrete a special fluid that coats the end like paint. With their tail in hand, Smeargle can draw anything, from simple streaks of color to works of art equivalent to any human artist. In the wild they create simple pieces as markings of territory or as moments of inspiration that had no other canvas around, and in groups they will coat their feet and plant them on each other’s back to leave footprints to celebrate the individual’s adulthood. They are the most dominant group of graffiti artists worldwide, greater even than the sibling species of Grafifi named after the art form._”

> “_The color of the fluid in their tails differentiates between each individual. While their color is mostly informed by that of their parent Smeargle’s, they are still influenced by their diets when they are young and will settle into a single color by puberty; there is short of one hundred different recorded colors known for Smearle to produce naturally. And there is speculation among Pokémon researchers that the organ which produces their natural paint can seep more than one color at a time, and that one day a Smeargle could be born who can paint in mutliple colors simultaneously._”
