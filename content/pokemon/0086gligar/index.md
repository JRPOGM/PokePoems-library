# Face Hugger

<blockquote>
I attempt to take in every detail

There is a dip beneath your left eye<br>
Where skin should be<br>
The brows above your eyes<br>
Are thinner on the outside<br>
The inside is more full<br>
More on the right than the left<br>
Four wrinkles lap up your lower lids<br>
Like the tail of a comet, all pointing to the starfall tear ducts<br>
A crook on the bridge of your nose<br>
Attempting to gather all your freckles<br>
There’s less gum over your left canine<br>
What a bad girl she’s been<br>
And the corners on the dip in your chin<br>
Bite back just as hard

Do you mind<br>
How enveloped in you I am?
</blockquote>

## Gligar, the Fly Scorpion Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/gligar.jpeg)

> “_Gligar do as their name implies; they cannot properly fly, but they do glide. Gligar make their nests at high elevation, usually on the corners of mountainsides or exceptionally tall trees. They jump from there, plummeting from the sky before slowing their descent with their wings, though they never let the wind carry them too far of a distance away from their nest. Because their wings connect to their legs and their tails protrude from lower on their body instead of their lower back, Gligar are unable to walk normally and instead have to hop around on all four when traversing flat land surfaces, though they are able to more naturally climb angled surfaces like trees and mountainsides to return to their nests just fine._”

> “_This high-altitude lifestyle is specifically a choice for hunting. Gligar sneak up on their prey by dropping on them from the sky, so living in high elevations gives them the time to stalk slowly in the air before diving down. Once they are confident their target does not suspect their approach, Gligar aim for the head. Their legs wrap around their prey’s neck and their clawed hands behind their head, wrapping their wings around their face and ears to all but suffocate their prey; because the goal isn’t to suffocate them, but to distract and blind them from their real plan. When their limbs are secured, Gligar stab the tip of their tail into their prey’s neck, injecting them with a paralytic that will kill most prey in under sixty seconds, or around thirty for especially small prey._”
