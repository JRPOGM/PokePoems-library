# Staring Ahead

<blockquote>
Blank eyes focused on the distance are not lost in their head<br>
They are not distracted by thoughts in their mind<br>
Or voices behind their ears<br>
They are not trapped within their own shell

Their eyes can see<br>
Through the bookshelves and biographies<br>
Past the drywall and brick<br>
Beyond streets and forests<br>
Outside the Tropo, Strato, Meso, Thermo and Exo<br>
Farther than Orion’s Arm, The Galactic Center and Sagittarius A<br>
Further than the Cigar, Sombrero, Tadpole and Hoag<br>
Yonder the membrane of existence’s edge<br>
Across the space between space<br>
And right into another

They are not eyes lost in thought<br>
But witnesses to another world<br>
Only seen in the blank stare
</blockquote>

## Natu, the Tiny Bird Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/natu.jpeg)

> “_Like the Spearow, Natu are not skilled flyers. They cannot fly at all, in fact, as their wings are not fully developed, so the species moves around by hopping and skipping on their two feet. In the absence of flight, this species has developed an impressive jump height, allowing the birds of small stature to leap up to branches taller than most humans. They only do this in search of food, pecking at the shoots and sprouts on branches, when it can’t find anything on the ground floor; otherwise, they will skip to get around or climb the trunks with their talons to gain height._”

> “_Disarmingly interesting about this species of Pokémon are their eyes. Wide and unblinking, staring into a Natu’s eyes can give the feeling that the Pokémon is staring into your soul. They will stare back, and they will not move or run away until you turn away first. It is not confidently known why they do this, but there are tales that their evolution, Xatu, is able to see into the future; therefore, it is believed this is the Natu displaying an early development of their evolution’s capabilities, attempted to see a person’s future by looking through their eyes._”
