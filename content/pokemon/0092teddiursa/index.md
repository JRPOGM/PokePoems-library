# Blood-Soaked Clay

<blockquote>
Sometimes, I think<br>
It is impossible for some men to change<br>
To wash their hands in the river<br>
To reshape the form they molded for themselves<br>
To avoid forever wearing the face of their past

You malleable few who try<br>
Throwing yourself onto the potter’s wheel<br>
Hoping your skin has not hardened<br>
That there is still something more you can be<br>
Yet of you already kilned<br>
Of you who are known as ceramic<br>
There is no hope in this shop

So much of you is chipped<br>
Holes in your shell<br>
Chunks missing from your skin<br>
Or portions overcooked<br>
You’ve lived on shelves too long to be material<br>
Seen by too many to be forgotten when you are removed<br>
You will be recognized the moment you step foot into the display<br>
No matter how much paint you dip yourself into

There is no erasing what is on your hands<br>
When all you’ve done is move them behind your back
</blockquote>

## Teddiursa, the Little Bear Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/teddiursa.jpeg)

> “_Some say there is no greater a lover of honey than Teddiursa. These small Pokémon are excellent hunters of the sappy treat, able to sniff out their sources naturally or stealthily follow other Pokémon like Combee or Beedrill to stake out their hive. They can be unnaturally quiet when they want to be, attempting to go unnoticed until the bee Pokémon leave their homes and leave an opening for the Teddiura to strike and steal as much honey as they can. The one detail that can ruin their hunts and expose their hiding spots are the crescent moons on their head, which can glow in the dark or simply light up according to the Teddiursa’s excitement, an emotion they regularly feel at the sight of their favorite food._”

> “_Because of how much Teddiursa eat between washing their bodies, their paws and fur are permanently stained with the flavors of the foods they eat; they can often be seen idly licking their paws when nothing is going on because they can taste the lingering flavor on them. The scent and taste of their palms differ between each individual, informed by their specific diets and the different foods they’ve succeeded in finding on their hunts. While unhealthy for many other species of Pokémon, not only does this not harm the damage their claws can do if they must fight, but having something to taste and feed on works as an emergency habit for Teddiursa who have been isolated from their families or who have been unsuccessful in their search for food, keeping their tension low as to not impact their ability to think and hunt._”
