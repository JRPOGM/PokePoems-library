# Forever-Echoing Screams

<blockquote>
Fear is a self-sustaining cycle<br>
The terrible shock the terrified<br>
Their screams encouraging their ability to scare<br>
And informing the world around of them<br>
The perception is perpetuated<br>
As the terrified attempt to avoid the terrified<br>
Encouraging them to chase the terrified<br>
One cannot exist without the other
</blockquote>

## Misdreavus, the Screech Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/misdreavus.jpeg)

> “_Similarly to the Gastly line, Misdreavus are playful creatures. They enjoy playing tricks and scaring others, mimicking the cries and sobs of human children to lure people close before leaping out to shock and surprise them. The startle and fear these actions put in people are an energy Misdreavus can feast on, absorbing the emotions into the beads around their neck so they can be transformed into a sustenance they can consume. In desperate measures of hunger, their actions will turn physical, attempting to rile up their targets for louder and more expressive reactions to feast on. Anyone who is unfazed by their attempts to scare unsettle and scare off the Misdreavus instead, believing them to be a dangerous creature that could cause them serious harm._”

> “_This behavior of theirs is solely informed by their need to survive. The energy they absorb and feast upon is their most abundant and renewable source of food, more than anything tangible, and one that fills them with more energy to grow stronger unlike standard food products and sources. Young Misdreavus spend most of their free time between scaring people in learning how to scare better, teaching and learning from their fellow Misdreavus the skills and tricks to get the best reaction out of their prey._”
