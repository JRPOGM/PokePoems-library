# Ladybird

<blockquote>
I am but one spot of many in her life<br>
Could be mistaken for nothing more than a fleck<br>
But not to her, it seems

No matter how small we few are<br>
She believes us important pieces of the whole<br>
Coincidentally, we are as much the Coccinellidae as she
</blockquote>

## Ledyba, the Five Star Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/ledyba.jpeg)

> “_Ledyba are cowardly creatures. This Bug-type species of Pokémon congregate in large lovelinesses, to huddle together for warmth and to find security in numbers when traveling in the wild. On one’s own, a Ledyba can be so overcome with fear that their limbs lock up and they find themselves unable to move, making them easy targets for many predator Pokémon. If confronted, they will not act offensively, instead attempting to set up Light Screens and Reflects to weaken any attacks directed at them. In colder climates and Winter seasons when the species would often aim to hibernate and hide out from the low temperatures, any individual left to their defenses will find their body temperature plummet and become too cold to move._”

> “_To balance out some of the struggles they face in the world, Ledyba have a second means of communicating with one another. Their bodies naturally secrete a harmless, strong-odor fluid. This sap can express emotions to others of their group, the aroma different depending on their moods; it can smell sweet when they are happy, sour when they are angry, and even putrid when they are distressed. Like the Sentret thumping their tails on the ground at the sight of danger, the aroma of their sap can act as a silent warning to the rest of their loveliness about their surroundings. And Ledyba use this sap as a marker for navigation and travel, smearing small clumps on trees and rocks they pass to leave a trail for potentially lost individuals to follow; or for individuals to leave as a dripping trail, so the hive can more confidently stick together to recollect one of their own._”
