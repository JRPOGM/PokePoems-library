## Remoraid, the Jet Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/remoraid.jpeg)

> “_Dubbed “Snipers of the Seas” by fishermen and Pokémon professors, Remoraid are proficient water guns given life. Sucking in water and shooting it out with their abdominal muscles, this Pokémon can shoot water at terrifying speeds, with terrifying accuracy, and at terrifying distances of up to three hundred feet away. They can sometimes be found challenging Clawitzer in a test of firepower against cliffsides and rocks. In precarious situations in which they cannot fight or cannot win, they instead use the kickback of their shots to propel their bodies backwards, quickly escaping confrontations at an impressive pace most other species of Pokémon cannot match._”

> “_Remoraid are most often seen accompanying a Pokémon they are not genetically tied to; the Kite Pokémon, Mantine. The smaller Jet Pokémon cling to the underside of the larger species, joining their travels across the sea idly to avoid their own foes and feast on the scraps of food the Mantine find first. They only travel in pairs, one Remoraid to any Mantine, and they do not leech off the other’s good nature as the smaller Pokémon will help the large creature in hunting as a second pair of eyes and in battle as a formidable ally._”
