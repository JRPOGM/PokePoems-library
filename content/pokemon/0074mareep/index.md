## Mareep, the Wool Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/mareep.jpeg)

> “_Mareep are adorably dangerous. Like many Electric types, Mareep generate their own electricity, each rubbing their wool against itself as they move around storing it in the fleece; the larger the charge they carry, the greater the volume of their fleece and the stronger the glow of their tail. The electricity can be dispersed through touch as equally as it could be fired out in an attack, and many things that collide with their charged wool will subsequently be shocked without the Mareep meaning to do so. They do not have an internal organ or sack in which to store their charge like many other Electric-type species, so the shearing of their wool is a practice that helps the Mareep stay healthy and protect those around them from an accidental shock._”

> “_Mareep wool processing in the fashion industry is handled by only a handful of companies. A Mareep’s fleece is capable of regulating their body temperature, as between the curls of wool they are able to create little air pockets that can distribute cold air in the summer months or hot air in the winter, so their wool became a point of interest to design year-round clothing that could do the same for humans. Because their wool holds their electricity, removing the fleece requires special tools and processing the material to lose the static charge before it can be fashioned into clothing and other household belongings is only possible through special, expensive equipment. Mareep farms shear and deliver the static wool in safe packaging to the same companies across the world, often signed to distribute to four at a time since the Mareep can regrow their fleece within a week after its removal._”
