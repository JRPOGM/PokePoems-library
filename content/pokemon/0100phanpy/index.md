# Clean Freak

<blockquote>
The clean freak,<br>
At the sight of murky eyes,<br>
From which they cannot parse dust or shine,<br>
Worry more than any other as to what they can do to help.
</blockquote>

## Phanpy, the Long Nose Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/phanpy.jpeg)

> “_Young Phanpy do not realize their own strength. Similarly to the Totodile, they are playful animals; childish and bright-eyed. They approach the world with a positive perception and take interest in that which they do not understand or that which they know pleases them greatly. When high on elation, however, Phanpy tend to throw their weight around without care, falling into the feeling of freedom and losing a sense of their surroundings. With their trunk alone they can swing through and smash bone in a single motion, and their bodies can barrel through stone walls with little struggle. Tending and raising a Phanpy requires discipline, attention, and easily replaceable objects._”

> “_They are also a bit of a clean freak. Phanpy demand a clean hide, even helping each other wash their bodies of dirt and sand when they are near a source of water and drying themselves off by flapping their great big ears. If they are dirty even slightly, they will know, and they will throw a dangerous tantrum until all stains and spots on them are washed off completely. This can lead to destructive mornings after they pull themselves from the holes they dig into the ground to sleep, which is why their parent Donphan always keep their herds close to riverbends when they travel._”
