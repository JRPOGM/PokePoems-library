# Mixer

<blockquote>
And the wallflower sit in the back<br>
Invisible beneath the crowd of clouds<br>
Hiding where the sun’s smile can’t catch him<br>
Yet still expecting to sprout

He is not prepared when the bee flies over<br>
Red cups mixed with pollen and punch<br>
Coming up just for a brush and a taste<br>
Before fluttering back to her hive

That is when the wallflower begins to bloom
</blockquote>

## Shuckle, the Mold Pokémon

![hand-drawn artwork](/images/shuckle.jpeg)

> “_Shuckle are one of the best defensive Pokémon species in recorded history. While offensively they are one of the weakest, Shuckle are the living example of ‘the best offense is a great defense.’ Virtually nothing can threaten these Pokémon with a single hit, as their thin bodies protruding from their rotund shell can quickly retract and leave their outer shells to eat most of the damage in their place. And any damage the Shuckle’s body does take, the Pokémon is prepared in advance to heal._”

> “_As their bodies do not take up the vast space inside their shells, Shuckle use the extra room to store berries and other foods. While they travel, these berries are slowly mushed by the movements and fermented by fluids secreted by a Shuckle’s skin. This turns the food into juices which the Pokémon can store in pockets on the inner wall of their shell, preserving the liquid for long periods of time until they are needed; this means in battle, the Mold Pokémon have concoctions to heal damage or status ailments when they are inflicted, prolonging the battle in an effort to tire out their opponents. This same juice concoction is also edible to other forms of life, and a few regions where they are naturally found often employ the species to mass produce their berry juice for the grocery market. Providing these blends for others does seem to please Shuckle greatly, so there is little fight put up when they are offered the job._”
