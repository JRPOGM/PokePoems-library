Gift-Giving

Present a present to the world
As though you had just decorated the pine tree

Everyone is worried about what the leave behind when they go
Immortalization and imprints they wish to control
A future of their existence persisting they wish to ensure
And what it needs to be to succeed

I believe, in its simplicity
All that is needed is something under wrapping and a bow
Something you would give to someone you would love
Without asking it to love you back

That will be remembered better than anything else

Delibird, the Delivery Pokémon

“Delibird are inherently generous creatures. With long tails grown from their feathers, this Pokémon can grab its end and fold their tail into a makeshift satchel of which they carry food with. They usually intend to bring what they find back to their nests, but that does not stop Delibird from handing off their food to whoever they pass when they believe it necessary; that, or they throw them like weapons at anyone who attacks them without thinking, and depending on the berries they throw they may inadvertently heal their opponents. But this Pokémon’s nature is to be kind and courteous first and foremost to those who are kind and are in need. Once, a wild Delibird aided a man in reaching the peak of Mount Coronet in Sinnoh.”
“Though an Ice-type Pokémon, Delibird are not bothered by warm weather. They often build their nests in places of high altitude and low temperatures, but they have no qualms or problems searching downhill or in tropical climates to find the food they want; some live across every island of Alola, even though Mount Lanakila is only located on Ula’Ula Island. This is how the species is able to bring small Bug Pokémon back to their nest as food for their chicks, as they are the only living organisms in the Pokémon’s diet. Because they think it normal and natural to themselves, Delibird will also gift the carcases of small Bug types to the people it comes across believing them suitable food for everyone.”
