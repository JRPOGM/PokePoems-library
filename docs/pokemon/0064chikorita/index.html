Putting Out

We release that which we cannot contain
For it poisons

Bottled emotions within vase skin
Pour out that which overflows
Or watch craftsmanship of coverups crack under pressure
In the end, everything spills

Fury
Rage
Sadness
Sorrow
Knowledge
Secrets
Conjoined weight is a punch to the gut
Words are the blood that slips out between the teeth

We withhold that which we cannot contain
For it contaminates

Anger like wildfire
Tears like tsunamis
The private-kept hopping from burning ears like rabbits

Infection
We attempt to avoid
Ache
For which we wish not to be the cause
Break
Do the thin threads between us
Fishing lines used to reel in friends
Going slack at the first sign of a fight

So too, is it
We put our hearts out into the world

Light shows of joy
Art of beauty
Music of serenity
That which pumps blood from one to the next

For it, too
Bleeds and stains others
Like water in soil
Paint on canvas
Heat through skin
And those thin wires between us
Knot together in solitude

Chikorita, the Leaf Pokémon

“Though they are a common species offered by Pokémon Professors to new Trainers starting their journeys through the world and challenge any region’s battling system, Chikorita are a docile species of Pokémon. They share a close relative to the Kanto region’s Grass-type starter, Bulbasaur, and like many other species of the same type they can sustain their body through exposure to direct sunlight. Sunlight keeps their body healthy and the leaf atop their head from withering. Chikorita use this protruding piece of their biology to interact with much of their surrounding environment, cutting through natural blockage when they travel or judging the air and temperatures in their immediate surroundings to avoid rising humidity that could impact their health. Said leaf will act like a compass pointer when they need to move, angled in the direction opposite of encroaching changes in the atmosphere.”
“Chikorita also have a natural amora they control the emission of, using the leaf atop their head to waft and spread the scent through the air faster if need be. They often use this when engaged in battles with other Pokémon, many of which greatly outclass the smaller Leaf Pokémon in size and power, thus they choose to rely on their natural aroma to escape safely. Said fragrance is harmless to those who inhale it, as it only succeeds in dulling aggression of those who breathe it in; this can give the Chikorita the opportunity to escape the sights of their docile assailant before their attitude can revert, though this is dependent on the size of their attacker changing how much of the aroma they must inhale for there to be any effect at all.”
