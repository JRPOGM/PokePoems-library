The Sheep in Wolf’s Clothing

It does not matter the barred fangs
Or the sharp claws
Do not care for the falling, foaming spit
Or the blood bleeding into their eyes

There is a man beneath the wild beast that walks
Burying itself under insecurity and ego masked as power
There fur is nothing but a stolen coat
You will know when you rip it from them

Snubbull, the Fairy Pokémon

“Snubbull are an easily recognizable species, as one of the only dog Pokémon species to walk only on their hind legs. Against their bright pink fur, short stature and roundish body, Snubbull do their best to look fierce, paws always at the ready in front of their chests and their lower fangs constantly on display with a growl forever building in their throat. Maintaining an intimidating expression helps to confront attackers and predators, challenging them at first sight and many times succeeding in convincing their opponents to back down before a fight can begin. One might look at their grumpy faces and truly believe them to be aggressive creatures.”
“But Snubbull are anything but aggressive. They are actually quite timid, their tactics to scare and intimidate others a mask over their strong fear of most any creature even slightly bigger than themselves. While they work to scare off Pokémon, knowing the true nature of the Snubbull made them endearing to many women who instead find them cute and adorable and, in taking care of them as pets and partners, have succeeded in getting the Snubbull to open up and express their true playful natures in the security of civilization. The only ones who abuse this knowledge are other domesticated dog Pokémon species, attempting to use their meek behaviors to their own advantages.”
