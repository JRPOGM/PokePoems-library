A Deer in Headlights

I branch out
Extend my hand and engage in embrace
Attempting to break out of this shell I call my life

That does not mean I’ve stopped watching
Every word
Every expression
Every choice
Theirs and mine
Because something will come up
Because I am sure of it
Because I am used to it
A line drawn in the sand
That splits the earth and draws up back apart
Puts us once again in worlds without overlap
I expect these moments to come barreling down the road
And like a deer in headlights
I will be there
To be run over
Or to run for the hills

Sentret, the Scout Pokémon

“A Sentret’s tail is quite tough, despite the plush appearance of the rest of the Pokémon’s body. Their hind appendage is firm, capable of supporting the body’s weight at a height above which they normally stand at on their hind legs. Like the Doduo’s aversion to fighting with their feet, Sentet do not use their tails for battle; instead, they are used to help scout their surroundings. As a pack species, Sentret appoint individuals who work as watchmen for the rest of them, propped up on their tails like watchtowers above the tall grass areas they live in.”
“While most Sentret will take up the role of guard, day or night, a few select from the pack work alongside them on their own legs. Instead of holding themselves up with their tails, these individuals wait for the scouts to spot an approaching predator or other danger. In most circumstances, the scout will give a shrill cry that alerts the pack to scatter and hide, but in cases where they cannot afford to make sound or they have not been spotted in return, their assistant Sentret will thump their tails against the ground, creating a quiet vibration that will send the same signal of danger through the pack.”
