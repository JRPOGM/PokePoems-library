Hands of War

Do I have a dominant hand because it was always better
Or because the other was never given the chance to try?

Upper-class status enforced
A choice forced to make
Though of the same earth, there is war
Difference instilled young
The pettiest of fights
Left or Right,
With which do you write?
Right or Left,
Which will be cleft?

Stomp your foot
Stand your ground
Push and pull until your knuckles pop
Till your fingers break
Until the bird is all you have to send a message
From being drafted to fight in this thumb war

To life, do you expect a right?
In death, what of you will be left?

Aipom, the Long Tail Pokémon

“Aipom are one of the only known species of Pokémon with exactly three hands. While they only have two arms, the bulbous end of their tails function just as well as their paws do, or even better than. With three long fingers the species can move and flex expertly, an Aipom’s tail is capable of holding their own body weight as they hand from trees, holding branches in a tight grip. They travel from tree to tree with their tails, swinging and throwing their bodies across and grabbing branch after branch with their enlarged appendage. They almost rarely use their arms and legs to move around, but they do often enough that the appendages remain when the species evolves into Ambipom.”
“But an Aipom’s survival is greatly dependent on their tails. They mostly exercise and train strictly on their tail’s usage, in climbing, swinging and crushing; the latter is trained the most to ensure the Aipom can feed itself, on berries or on prey. They wrap the entirety of their tails around branches when they sleep, preventing their bodies from plummeting to the ground if they are ever bothered in the night. And as a long-lived species, they’ve existed alongside humanity for hundreds of years, adapting their tails to perform actions humans do, effectively changing their natural lifestyle to one that now mirrors our own in several ways.”
