Storm Fall

Wind beats on the door
Alcohol carried through the breeze
And I do not open the door

Thunder booms in his voice
Lightning cracking his fist against his chest
And I will not open the door

Rain is a performance
Puddles of mud that only dirty the souls of his shoes
And I shall not open the door

The storm is drunk on power
For it believes itself strong
Yet it cannot open the door, either

Yanma, the Clear Wing Pokémon

“Yanma are a species better capable at flight than most Flying-type Pokémon, in coordination and raw power behind each flap of their wings. Yanma can stop midair almost instantaneously, turn on a dime quickly, and continue flying at top speed in a new direction within the span of only a few seconds. Their set of four see-through wings, two on either side of their body, flap with such speed and intensity that they can kick up winds strong enough to shatter glass at close range in under five seconds, and in the past could blow apart cabin houses in the rare occurrence that they flew in a group.”
“Like several other Bug-type species, Yanma also have incredible sight. The visor-like shape over their eyes functions as a magnifier for the species, also allowing them a near full 360 vision at all times, at their control and discretion. This makes Yanma excellent hunters of prey, able to spot their targets from any angle when at high altitude before repositioning their bodies to quickly dive at their prey. This also helps them shoo off other Yanma passing through their territory, as the species live mostly in isolation.”
