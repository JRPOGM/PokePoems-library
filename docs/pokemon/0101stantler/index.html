Tarnished Beauty

The elk with velvet fur
Prances through the redwood
Feeding on the bark

The elk with the zebra-striped hide
Dashes through the birch
His antlers branching from his skull

The elk with the verdant skin
Rests in the underbush
Growing fawn on the lawn

The elk with the oak legs
Stands tall in the forest
Staring down the barrel of a gun
As the hunter stares back
Waiting to watch him run

Stantler, the Big Horn Pokémon

“Once upon a time, Stantler may have been a Psychic-type Pokémon species. Back when the world and nature were harsher, when people and Pokémon had yet to properly coexist as they do today, the species of Big Horn Pokémon were capable of evolution, a reward for their survival in battle and their show of strength. As a Wyrdeer, their antlers harness reality-warping psychic powers, constantly morphing the air around them that one could peer between their antlers and see into another dimension.”
“In the calmer modern day, Stantler have lost that edge of power. They can now no longer evolve under today’s conditions, bred for generations as a species who could maintain a single form throughout their lifetime on the range of power they possess as is. Though diminished, their antlers do still hold mysterious powers and capabilities of inducing confusion on those who stare into them too long. One of the only things of them that have maintained over the centuries are the black orbs in the base of their antlers, remarkably removable without harm and can be crushed into a fine powder for medicinal uses like sleep aid. Hunters and collectors marvel for Stantler’s antlers, and their population has struggled to maintain itself in several regions across the world without their previous ability to achieve a higher power to defend themselves.”
