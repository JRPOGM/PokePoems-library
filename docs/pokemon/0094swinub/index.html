Fairy Rings

Toadstools dance in the scorched grass trailing behind the dragon’s tail
The jig bug prancing on their flat tops
A plan in brew, stirring in grasshopper strings

An eternal song of enchantment and entrapment
As sung by the fairies of old
With a band of whistling grass blades
And wind drumming on tree trunks cold

To ensnare all those who enter the ring
To feed the dragon king
So the land may burn and the mushrooms may grow
So the next trap may be laid in the Spring

Swinub, the Pig Pokémon

“The fur of a Swinub covers almost every inch of their body from view, except for the pink nose on the front of their face. They are excellent seekers, using their sense of scent to follow and locate certain aromas whether they are hunting or relaxing. Even in colder climates and strong storms, Swinub can press their nose to the ground and follow the lingering traces of another’s scent. Be it dirt, sand or ice, these Pokémon can track the trails of anything anywhere better than almost any species.”
“Swinub enjoy hunting for mushrooms at their own leisure, most specifically the fairy rings made of the Crowned Bonnet mushrooms grown in the Crown Tundra of Galar, but they have no real qualms over which source of food they come across. Under human care, many use the Swinub’s scent to provide search and rescue for those who may be lost in tundra areas around the world. When they evolve into Piloswine or Mamoswine their bodies can be used to clear high inches of snow to make paths in the storms, but in the Swinub stage they can sniff out the trails of people through the snow or anyone who may be trapped beneath it.”
