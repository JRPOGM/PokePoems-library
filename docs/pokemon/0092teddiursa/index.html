Blood-Soaked Clay

Sometimes, I think
It is impossible for some men to change
To wash their hands in the river
To reshape the form they molded for themselves
To avoid forever wearing the face of their past

You malleable few who try
Throwing yourself onto the potter’s wheel
Hoping your skin has not hardened
That there is still something more you can be
Yet of you already kilned
Of you who are known as ceramic
There is no hope in this shop

So much of you is chipped
Holes in your shell
Chunks missing from your skin
Or portions overcooked
You’ve lived on shelves too long to be material
Seen by too many to be forgotten when you are removed
You will be recognized the moment you step foot into the display
No matter how much paint you dip yourself into

There is no erasing what is on your hands
When all you’ve done is move them behind your back

Teddiursa, the Little Bear Pokémon

“Some say there is no greater a lover of honey than Teddiursa. These small Pokémon are excellent hunters of the sappy treat, able to sniff out their sources naturally or stealthily follow other Pokémon like Combee or Beedrill to stake out their hive. They can be unnaturally quiet when they want to be, attempting to go unnoticed until the bee Pokémon leave their homes and leave an opening for the Teddiura to strike and steal as much honey as they can. The one detail that can ruin their hunts and expose their hiding spots are the crescent moons on their head, which can glow in the dark or simply light up according to the Teddiursa’s excitement, an emotion they regularly feel at the sight of their favorite food.”
“Because of how much Teddiursa eat between washing their bodies, their paws and fur are permanently stained with the flavors of the foods they eat; they can often be seen idly licking their paws when nothing is going on because they can taste the lingering flavor on them. The scent and taste of their palms differ between each individual, informed by their specific diets and the different foods they’ve succeeded in finding on their hunts. While unhealthy for many other species of Pokémon, not only does this not harm the damage their claws can do if they must fight, but having something to taste and feed on works as an emergency habit for Teddiursa who have been isolated from their families or who have been unsuccessful in their search for food, keeping their tension low as to not impact their ability to think and hunt.”
