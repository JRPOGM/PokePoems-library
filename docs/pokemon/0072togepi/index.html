Walking on Egg Shells

Maybe the universe hatched from an egg
The Big Bang was no more than boiling yolk
Stars sizzling alive, oil on the pan

So too do chicks, featherless and pale
Chirp solar flares at the first sight of the sun

As too do ideas
Henhouses of thoughts, chefs in the synapses
One laying eggs as the other cracks them open for answers

Yet here come the men
Who are not farmers or cooks
Walking on egg shells
Smashing history beneath their boots
Taking what was born as their own
Burying connective tissue between creator and creation
There is no playing it safe with men like these
There is only defending your flock

Togepi, the Spikeball Pokémon

“Several species of Pokémon have myths and fairytales about bringing good fortune to those who cross their paths, but Togepi’s tales center on dedication to raise one. One of the few species under the classification of ‘Baby Pokémon,’ short of stature and often mistaken for a standard Pokémon egg when curled up, Togepi are mostly incapable of protecting themselves. They require evolution to get stronger and older, unlike most species of Pokémon who can live their whole lives in their first stage without evolving and still grow old. They will only achieve evolution by absorbing the positive and caring energy from those around them.”
“Thus Togepi became one of many species of Pokémon adult humans care for as though they were human children, either alongside their own or as practice to prepare for having children of their own, and from there did the rumors of Togepi’s blessings begin. By caring for them like a child, helping the species evolve and grow, people came to believe doing such would be returned to them in kind later in life. Togepi as they are, are believed to have the capability to disperse the love they’ve been given as a blessing of luck to others, and when they evolve into Togetic and again into Togekiss that power to bring fortune is increased tenfold. It is unsure if this is simply a placebo effect of forming a caring bond for a Pokémon who in turn attempts to take care of their ‘parent’ thus putting them in favorable positions in life, but so long as the relationships are peaceful and helpful the rumors and myths continue to spread.”
