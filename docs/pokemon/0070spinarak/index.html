A Spider Spinning Its Own Silk

Silk spun from expectation
Preparation strung up like a star
He waits

The spider is a man of patience
A man with the knowledge of time
And understanding of habit
Cycles and patterns he can predict
So precise he is mistaken for a psychic

And he can, see the future
As his eyes have seen the past
The real mystery is
Was he the one who spun the ouroboros?

Spinarak, the String Spit Pokémon

“A spun web can be considered a Spinarak’s second nervous system. These arachnid Pokémon are patient hunters, weaving webs of fine but durable thread where they know others to pass through and sitting quietly at the corner of their work for another creature to stumble into. Spinarak can judge their prey through the vibrations of their webs when they struggle, parsing size, shape and potential strength before they even set eyes on their victims. Despite the variety they can capture, Spinarak prefer smaller prey like Cutiefly, of which they can more easily subdue with the weak but potent venom in their fangs to feast on them.”
“They have also found life as employed hands in human society. Several Bug-type species worldwide are captured and trained to work in the commercial industry, their threads worked and woven into clothing, tools and other goods to diversify the market; the Sewaddle species use their webs and leaves to create a series of natural-material fashion popular in the Unova region. Spinarak’s silk is used to create nets for hunters and fishermen, that do not degrade as quickly as other brands and maintain the captive stickiness that makes the Pokémon’s webs so strong.”
