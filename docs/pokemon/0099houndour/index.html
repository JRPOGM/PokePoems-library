The Versatility of the Human Aqueduct

The wailing whales
Brooding over the loss of their brood

Crocodiles tearing tears
From the plovers that pull over to help them

Bogus buoyant sympathies
When the heart can’t find the heart to ache

How the joyous join
And washed up flounder under shore and scorn

All bodies of water trickling from the same source
Yet mankind has come to tell them apart

Houndour, the Dark Pokémon

“Houndour are vocal creatures. They travel in packs, hunters making the majority of their numbers, and every action they take are informed by the voice of another. Packs of Houndour have a language all of their own, where their cries and their barks and their howls each signify a different emotion, command, or piece of information only others of their pack would understand. The only universal sound between packs and families is the lone cry that the others wake to, to inform them all of the new day. They are often loudest when they hunt, howling through the air to inform the others of their position and of the prey they are chasing together; with spacial coordination, Houndour use each other’s cries to scare their target into a specific path in which they can corner them.”
“As domesticated Pokémon, Houndour attune their cries for their owners and fellow Pokémon to understand them as best they can. While not perfect mimics, they will learn the sounds and pitches of other civilized species and creatures, and will match them as closely as possible with their own voice to convey the same emotions. They will also develop new sounds and signals specifically for their Trainers and owners, a personal means of communication they know is understood and thus will only use with the individual that they have bonded closest with.”
