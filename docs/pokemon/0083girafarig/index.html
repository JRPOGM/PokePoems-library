Was There Ever A Mask?

Everyone has a second face
A side to them no one can see
A voice only they can hear
A hand attempting to reach
What they should not be able

They are invisible
Not because they are hidden
Or consciously obstructed
But because no one is looking
For something that shouldn't be

But someone, someday, will
Watch skin slip over
Light flicker colors
And meet somebody new
Going by the same name

And from away, someone will watch
Every bounce of the mouth
Every flinch of the hand
And not a thing thought out of place
When it's standing in the same

Girafarig, the Long Neck Pokémon

“Among all species of Pokémon with multiple heads, brains and consciousnesses, Girafarig is only partially included in this classification. They have a dominant head similar to the Hydreigon species, and while their tail is technically a head and brain of its own, it does not have nearly the same level of conscious control as the Pokémon’s main personality. There is a small brain in the end of their tails, connected by a stem to their real head’s brain and only the muscles in the tail, no other organs. The mouth shape of its end mostly responds when the Girafarig eats, their jaws moving in sync even though only one can digest food.”
“The tail does contain some of the Pokémon’s psychic power itself. While most of its actions are completely reactionary, moving to the command of the Girafarig’s main body or to any other creature that gets close enough for it to bite at, the dots that resemble eyes can emit a pulse that induces a hypnotic state at anyone who returns its sight. It is not a brain that needs sleep or rest, so when the main body is unconscious, it remains attentive and watchful as its protector. It may not be as strong with its power as the main body, but it cares for their life just as much as their own, even in their forfeited control.”
