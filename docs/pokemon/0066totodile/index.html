Mind, Your Mouth

Watching what you say
Is not a subtitle life;
It is the writer’s.

Words are published the second they hit the ear,
Ink drying on the brain.
That is not paper one can burn,
Cumpled up and thrown away.

Even the final print another hears
Cannot erase that which was drafted before.
Ideas and attempts, unpleasant before they are satisfactory,
The written and the spoken, phrased before they are meant,
Seared in memory.

Watch that which you say.
Know how to handle a pen
Before you speak it.

Mind your mouth.

Totodile, the Big Jaw Pokémon

“Totodile are incredibly dangerous, despite their presented behaviors and attitude. Though small, members of this species have a greater strength than they initially realize. The youngest members of the species are inherently interested in the wider world, distracted by much and keen on interacting with it all. Pokémon breeders liken their perception of the world to that similar to young Dog Pokémon like Yamper and Growlithe -- of a naive interest in everything and a single idea of how to understand it.”
“The problem arises in how they choose to interact with it all. A Totodile’s strength is focused on their jaw, as they have sharp teeth and the bite strength to lock onto anything they sink their teeth into. This also means they have the strength to tear off anything weaker in density than can handle their fangs, and this puts many potential Trainers and other people and Pokémon in harm’s way attempting to care for the Big Jaw Pokémon. As the species is quite popular with Pokémon Professors as a First Partner Pokémon for new Trainers, those who do distribute them are legally required to train their behavior and withhold their power for a few years prior to entrusting them to a younger generation.”
