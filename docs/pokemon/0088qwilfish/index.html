The Sheep in Wolf’s Clothing 2

You puff out your chest
Making yourself big
Putting in all your energy
Pretending to be strong

Foam bubbling around the fangs
Howls growl in the back of your throat
Threatening the power that you can tear into theirs
But you never step forward

It’s all setting up for the fall
The day the facade melts
Exposing your true tender self
To all those you were trying to scare away

Do not fear to be honest from the start
Do not believe yourself small from the beginning
Be brave then and there
And then you can never fall

Qwilfish, the Balloon Pokémon

“Qwilfish are tricky to approach. Their bodies are littered with poisonous thorns grown from the scales across their body, giving them a handy tool for both offensive and defensive tasks. Most other fish Pokémon cannot get too close without risking their own bodies being injected with the dangerous poison the Qwilfish carry. In battle, Qwilfish can use their spikes to attack their opponents at a distance; by swallowing in large amounts of water or air, they can inflate their bodies rapidly, and their thorns will shoot off like bullets indiscriminately. The moments after is the clearest window anyone has to risk close proximity with the then deflating Qwilfish, and it is how most trained fisherman catch them after first triggering their instincts in battle.”
“But this ability to shoot their thorns from their body has only been a skill of theirs for the past few hundred years. Longer ago, a breed of Qwilfish existed, predominantly in the land of ancient Sinnoh, whose thorns were larger and fixed to their scales. Due to their diet at the time, these Qwilfish often parsed the toxins from their food into their own supply, also influencing their darker scales and larger tails. This allowed the Qwilfish to carry a more potent toxin that would incapacitate those they scratched for thrice as long as they do now. ”
