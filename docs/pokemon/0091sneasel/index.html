Hooked On You

It is impossible not to bleed
Where your claws dig in to my flesh
Inevitable that I melt
Under the heat of your breath
Unquestionable that I breathe
Only because you allow it

I will never have enough of you
Never as much as you have of me

Sneasel, the Sharp Claw Pokémon

“A Sneasel’s claws are designed for hunting. Sharp and extendable, not only can the species pierce through bark and rock but they can dice their prey apart with relative ease. These Pokémon hunt for prey and food at high elevations, using their claws to climb trees and mountains in search of nests of bird and bird-adjacent Pokémon. While a Sneasel would not complain about taking home a feathered target as food for their park, it is the eggs left in the nests they prefer most. As Sandshrew are one of the few species whose skin and hide can take a beating from their claws, most avian Pokémon are unable to stop Sneasel who get too close from taking their eggs. Many Pokémon Breeders have to carry defensely-trained Pokémon with them at all times to ensure the eggs they look after do not meet a similar fate.”
“These hunting tactics of Sneasel are barely changed from how they acted centuries ago, even when their biology was starkly different in its capabilities. Sneasel of the past lived solely in mountainous regions, so their claws were longer and more curved to aid in rock climbing. While this meant their striking power with their scratches were less refined and harder to pull off cleanly, they made up for this in having a natural poison to coat their hands with, one which would target the nerves of their prey if successfully cut, leaving them paralyzed and vulnerable for the Sneasel to finish off without further struggle.”
