Drifter

I go as the wind takes me
Unstable in my footsteps
Unable to hold ground
As imagination whisks me away

Blessing and curse
As I drift from shore to shore

Docking for a day
To spend my nights traveling beneath starlight

No land to call my own
No place to call home

Not as the wind keeps rolling
Possibilities floating by in the clouds
And I am left to chase after my dreams in the cumulative cumulus

Hoppip, the Cottonweed Pokémon

“It would be misguided to say Hoppip are endangered by the wind. It is true they are a light-mass species, able to float in the air thanks to the steady rotation of the leaves atop their head as a propeller and catch a light breeze to stay afloat even longer. Any speed of wind could lift this Pokémon off the ground against their will, so many Hoppip can be seen clutching blades of grass during moments of strong winds. Were they to let go, the breeze could carry them for miles or more before they could touch down again.”
“But Hoppip are not truly bothered by this. Many more willingly give in to the wind, letting it carry them wherever the breeze goes, carrying them across regions and even overseas. Hoppip are known to migrate worldwide multiple times a year, most often during the change of the seasons, simply because they wish to ride the weather when it comes. This only really proves difficult in understand where any one flock or individual originates from, as there is only one known species of Hoppip seen across every region; it is likelier the same Hoppip will have visited every region in the world before anyone can determine their point of origin.”
